Why this order: the field pass (0.8.1) closes the seams the shipped

deepen the life/illness story. **0.8.4 is a trial, not a promise** —

other, run test trials, and only implement if it succeeds; if the
engine's patterns can't carry it, it degrades to a documented
finding and the run moves on. **0.8.5 MCM comes after the trial so
its page is built once** — the interaction knobs (`sim.interact.*`)
are on the page the day it ships if the trial proved, and if the
trial failed the page simply has no interaction keys, zero rework.
**0.8.6a is the honest audit** (ReleasePlan's cut list revisited
against what exists now, with the trial's outcome and MCM both
weighable); 0.8.6b applies it. 0.8.6c is the hard gate. The 0.9.x
run is content and polish toward the Nexus beta: 0.9.1 grows the
content (dialog pools, timings, the birth journey), 0.9.2 presents
it (animations, final touches, the release).

**Audit candidate — everyone earns caps (field finding 2026-08-14).**
The 0.8.3 verify exposed a real economy hole: only stall-keepers
earn. Caravan workers, provisioners, and unemployed settlers spend
caps on meals and get nothing back, so they go permanently broke
and a sickness without caps means rest, not treatment (the log
showed the same handful of caravan workers `sick but broke` all
session). The honest fix belongs to the 0.8.6a audit: a wage or
piecework income (settlement jobs, caravan routes, a finder's cut
from the stall), so a working mind's pouch slowly refills and the
broke-gate stays meaningful instead of permanent. Everything rides
existing surface (pouch += N, no new engine).

## 0.8.1 — The Illness Field Pass

**Status: SHIPPED 2026-08-13.** All four seams closed and verified in
sessions: the economy balance held (12 buys, 1 broke in the first
outbreak; 126 contracted / 0 died); the global cough gate
(`sim.illness.coughGlobal`) and burst-paced news
(`sim.illness.newsMax`/`newsInterval`) landed; the wound window was
verified by the numbers (seed 0.5 crosses the cap at 250 s inside the
300 s hold — 40 s rescue window, medicine turns it); the child fix
(contagion-child deaths were the field finding — `childMult 1.1` +
real corpse on illness death) shipped with the 0.8.4 core rebuild.
The kin-gate per-second log flood and the verify channel's volume
(cadence 5.0, action-stable refusal keys) were the final polish.

**Goal:** close the seams the natural-radstorm test exposed.

1. **Illness economy balance** — 25 caps per dose, the recovery
   window, and the medicine price vs typical pouch wealth. The day-12
   test showed 73 buys; confirm the market cure doesn't make illness
   a non-event, and that the broke-rest path is the honest minority.
2. **The cough at outbreak scale** — MTCoughing every 12 s per sick
   mind is a flood in a settlement-wide outbreak; verify the rate-
   limit holds and the tell stays audible without spamming.
3. **Illness news cadence** — "X is ill" once per sickness is the
   design, but a 50-sick outbreak must not swamp the radio; confirm
   the announce set holds at scale.
4. **Wound vector sanity** — an untreated wound is the only death
   that should be earned; verify the 40 s rescue window reads right
   in game (medicine still turns it around).

**Files:** `src/Tuning.h` + `config/TheLivingCommonwealth.ini` (any
knob the balance finds), `src/Adapter.cpp` (the cough/news throttles).

**Verification:** a forced radstorm day (or a natural one — the
Commonwealth obliges): the sick cough at a sane cadence, the radio
announces without flooding, the broke-rest path appears, and a wound
victim with caps survives. Mid-outbreak save/load already locks.

## 0.8.2 — The Burial

**Status: VERIFIED 2026-08-14** — a kill in Sanctuary grieved
(5 minds), the corpse was laid to rest on schedule (`burials: the
settlement laid Nico Jackson to rest.`), and the ledger entry cleared
on the next co-save write. Co-save
v8 carries the burial book (dead form id + day died); the adapter
records each death at booking (`m_Burials`), restores the book across
save/load, and the per-second `BurialSweep` disables the corpse ref
(the game's own `TESObjectREFR::Disable()`) once `sim.death.burialDays`
(3) pass, logs `the settlement laid X to rest`, and pushes the news
line. A corpse whose cell is unloaded (or already cleaned up by the
game) settles the book without a scene — nothing to bury is honest.
The round-trip (form id + day) is locked in CoSaveTest; harness 26/26.

**Goal:** the corpse must not linger forever (ADR-0023's planned
stone). The sim's death path is complete in the books — death fact,
grief, legacy — but the game corpse stays in the settlement cell
forever (no cell reset there). After the mourning window
(`sim.death.burialDays`, INI-tunable), the adapter disables the
corpse ref and logs `the settlement laid X to rest` (+ a news line).
Body state is game state — adapter-owned, no engine change.

**Files:** `src/Adapter.cpp` (the burial sweep), `src/Tuning.h` +
the INI (`sim.death.burialDays`), `src/CoSave.{h,cpp}` (v8).

**Verification:** kill a settler, wait out the window, the body is

## 0.8.3 — The Sick Household

**Status: VERIFIED 2026-08-14.** Medicine is a stocked good and a
family cares for its own.

**In-game:** the self-buy fired (Caravan Worker Alexander bought a
25-cap dose from Provisioner Ivy — `the hold ends, recovery
begins`); the broke-gate fired repeatedly (`sick but broke — they
rest instead`); the shelves survived save/load (`2 market shelfs
restored from the co-save`) across three loads. The sell-out and the
family care-buy are the same `buyDose` path with their own
trigger conditions (a drained shelf, a sick spouse/child) —
reachable and code-locked, they surface in normal play when an
outbreak outruns a shelf or a married settler falls ill. Also fixed
in the field this session: the stale-save decay flood (restore now
re-derives rates from today's INI × the mind's jitter) and the
per-frame refusal spam (sim.walk.refusalCooldown parks a refused
mind).

- **Stocked medicine (co-save v9).** Each market's doses left today
  ride the co-save as (market form id, stock) pairs; a shelf with no
  entry reads as full (`sim.illness.stock`, default 10, INI), so a
  fresh world or a pre-v9 restore simply has full shelves. The
  buy path (`ReportArrival`) checks the shelf before the wallet: a
  sick mind at a sold-out stall rests instead (`finds the stall out
  of medicine`). Every market-open transition (the world-facts push)
  refills every known shelf — an outbreak can outrun a single day's
  stock, and a stall that sold out stays sold out across save/load
  until the next market day.
- **The family care-buy.** The trade block's dose logic is now one
  `buyDose` path used twice: self first (the existing 0.8.0
  behavior — the sick at the bench dose themselves), then, for a
  well buyer, the household: the spouse first (the design's
  headline), then a sick child — the ChildMult's promise, a child
  has no walk of its own to the bench, so a parent's trip is the
  dose. The shared household wallet pays either way (`PouchOf`), the
  seller's pouch grows, and the news reads `X bought medicine for
  Y — a family cares.`
- **Locked by test.** CoSaveTest carries a sold-down shelf
  (market 000250FE, 4 doses) through Encode → Decode and checks it
  comes back exactly; harness 26/26.

**Files:** the stall/trade path in `src/Adapter.cpp`, `src/CoSave
.{h,cpp}` (v9), `src/Illness.h` (`Stock`), `src/Tuning.h` + the INI
(`sim.illness.stock`), `src/main.cpp` (the glue).

**Verification:** a big outbreak drains the stalls and the sick wait
for tomorrow; a married sick settler gets dosed by the spouse's trip
to the bench; a sick child gets dosed by a parent's trip. Save/load
mid-sell-out: the stall stays empty until the next market day.

## 0.8.4 — Random Interactions (the trial)

**Goal:** settlers interact with each other unprompted — not only
when hunger drives them to a bench. The trial first: watch what the
existing sim already produces (trades at person-markets, rows at the
bench, the Socialize intent, proximity), run test trials, and
implement only what succeeds. The engine's patterns (Social/Trade
channels, `Say`, proximity reads) are the raw material; if the trial
shows the sim can't carry unprompted interaction cleanly, the stone
degrades to a documented finding and the run moves on.

**Status: BUILT 2026-08-14** — awaiting the in-game trial.

**What's built.** A proximity pass at the end of the per-second tick
(`Adapter::InteractPass`): loaded human minds gather their positions
once, and each cooldown-expired, non-walking mind finds its nearest
non-walking neighbour inside `sim.interact.radius` (default 400
units — a body's throw), rolls `sim.interact.chance` (0.4), and
speaks. The pool follows the bond via `Bonds::CurrentKind`: family
for spouse/sweetheart/friend, a quiet row for enemy/rival,
greet/gossip (30/70) for strangers. A walking mind never talks — the
interaction never interrupts a walk; a jittered cadence (0.5–1.5×
`sim.interact.cadence`, default 30 s) keeps a pair from lockstep
chatter. All quiet (`Say` without the loud flag): the lines read in
the log and the radio feed, not as HUD pops.

**Files:** `src/Adapter.cpp` (`InteractPass` + the tick hook),
`src/Adapter.h` (the cooldown map), `src/Tuning.h` + the INI
(`sim.interact.cadence/radius/chance`), `Dialogue.h` (the pools it
speaks). Harness 26/26 green.

**Verification:** two settlers who cross paths sometimes greet, talk,
or trade without a hunger drive; the interaction is rate-limited,
never spammy, and doesn't interrupt a walk. If the trial fails, the
finding is written and the stone is closed as a cut. Its outcome
feeds the MCM page (0.8.5) and the audit (0.8.6a).
