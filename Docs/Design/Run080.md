## The run, in build order

Each stone is verified in-game before the next — the standing rule.
The run is anchored at one end by what shipped (Illness & Medicine)
and at the other by the release gate (0.9.0). The four gate items
from the original 0.9.0 plan (scale, trust, docs, news) are pulled
forward into the run as real stones; 0.9.0 then re-verifies them all
in one pass and signs off.


Why this order: the field pass (0.8.1) closes the seams the shipped
stone exposed — the natural first move. The burial (0.8.2) and the
sick household (0.8.3) deepen the life/illness story the player just
saw. MCM (0.8.4) is the tuning convenience the docs deferred. The
news, scale, trust, and docs stones (0.8.5–0.8.8) are the release
gate's real content, built as proper stones instead of a checklist.
0.8.9 is the sweep before the gate verifies everything together.

## 0.8.1 — The Illness Field Pass

**Goal:** close the seams the natural-radstorm test exposed.

1. **Illness economy balance** — 25 caps per dose, the recovery
   window, and the medicine price vs typical pouch wealth. The day-12
   test showed 73 buys; confirm the market cure doesn't make illness
   a non-event, and that the broke-rest path is the honest minority.
2. **The cough at outbreak scale** — MTCoughing every 12 s per sick
   mind is a flood in a settlement-wide outbreak; verify the rate-
   limit holds and the tell stays audible without spamming.
3. **Illness news cadence** — "X is ill" once per sickness is the
   design, but a 50-sick outbreak must not swamp the radio; confirm
   the announce set holds at scale.
4. **Wound vector sanity** — an untreated wound is the only death
   that should be earned; verify the 40 s rescue window reads right
   in game (medicine still turns it around).

**Files:** `src/Tuning.h` + `config/TheLivingCommonwealth.ini` (any
knob the balance finds), `src/Adapter.cpp` (the cough/news throttles).

**Verification:** a forced radstorm day (or a natural one — the
Commonwealth obliges): the sick cough at a sane cadence, the radio
announces without flooding, the broke-rest path appears, and a wound
victim with caps survives. Mid-outbreak save/load already locks.

## 0.8.2 — The Burial

**Goal:** the corpse must not linger forever (ADR-0023's planned
stone). The sim's death path is complete in the books — death fact,
grief, legacy — but the game corpse stays in the settlement cell
forever (no cell reset there). After the mourning window
(`sim.death.burialDays`, INI-tunable), the adapter disables the
corpse ref and logs `the settlement laid X to rest` (+ a news line).
Body state is game state — adapter-owned, no engine change.

**Files:** `src/Adapter.cpp` (the burial sweep), `src/Tuning.h` +
the INI (`sim.death.burialDays`).

**Verification:** kill a settler, wait out the window, the body is
gone and the settlement heard it. Save/load across the burial: a
buried corpse stays buried.

## 0.8.3 — The Sick Household

**Goal:** the economy stone's next step — medicine is not an endless
shelf, and a family cares for its own.

1. **Stocked medicine.** Stalls stock a limited supply per day
   (`sim.illness.stock`, INI) that sells out, so an outbreak can
   outrun the market — the broke-rest path and the honest season
   return. Replenishes the next market day.
2. **The family buys for the sick.** The shared wallet already
   exists; when one spouse is ill, the other's market trip can buy
   the dose for the sick one — a care check on the household.

**Files:** the stall/trade path, `Households` (the care check),
`src/Tuning.h` + the INI (`sim.illness.stock`).

**Verification:** a big outbreak drains the stalls and the sick wait
for tomorrow; a married sick settler gets dosed by the spouse's trip
to the bench.

## 0.8.4 — MCM + Settings Manager

**Goal:** the player tunes in-game (Life.md's deferred player-window
half). A Mod Configuration Menu page exposing the tuning keys —
hunger rhythm, market hours, bond thresholds, births gate, illness
curve, gossip radius, population cap. The INI stays the source of
truth; MCM reads and writes it (or an override file), with a
restore-defaults button. The MCM mod is a **soft** dependency; a
small Papyrus surface on the adapter side.

**Files:** a Papyrus/MCM page (author-side asset), the adapter's
settings surface it calls.

**Verification:** an MCM change survives reload; defaults restore
cleanly; with no MCM installed, the INI alone still works.

## 0.8.5 — The Player's Stage

**Goal:** news where the player is. The feed prioritizes the
player's settlement — what happens where the player stands comes
first, the wider Commonwealth second — and the throttle stays sane
at 600 minds (one headline per beat, never a wall).

**Files:** the news feed + prioritization, the throttle.

**Verification:** standing in Sanctuary, the headlines are
Sanctuary's; the feed never floods during a mass event.

## 0.8.6 — Scale in the Field

**Goal:** the hard gate, measured. The engine proved 5000 minds
round-trip and year-long soaks; the adapter must prove a normal save
with hundreds of settlers ticks inside a frame budget. The engine's
`TickReport` (once a minute) measures it. A release that chugs at
600 minds is dead on arrival.

**Files:** the tick path, whatever the report names.

**Verification:** a restored 600+ mind save holds frame time over a
long session; any hotspot found is fixed or gated behind a knob.

## 0.8.7 — The Trust Story

**Goal:** remove-the-DLL is safe. The co-save rides in the save; the
save still loads without the mod; clean migration; no stale-record
corruption. The save/load proof is the mod's reputation.

**Files:** docs + whatever the proof run names.

**Verification:** load a saved world with the DLL removed, then
reinstall — nothing corrupts, nothing explodes, the world resumes.
