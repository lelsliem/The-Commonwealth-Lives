it (animations, final touches, the release).

**Audit candidate — everyone earns caps (field finding 2026-08-14).**
The 0.8.3 verify exposed a real economy hole: only stall-keepers
earn. Caravan workers, provisioners, and unemployed settlers spend
caps on meals and get nothing back, so they go permanently broke
and a sickness without caps means rest, not treatment (the log
showed the same handful of caravan workers `sick but broke` all
session). The honest fix belongs to the 0.8.6a audit: a wage or
piecework income (settlement jobs, caravan routes, a finder's cut
from the stall), so a working mind's pouch slowly refills and the
broke-gate stays meaningful instead of permanent. Everything rides
existing surface (pouch += N, no new engine).

family cares for its own.

**In-game:** the self-buy fired (Caravan Worker Alexander bought a

- **Stocked medicine (co-save v9).** Each market's doses left today

  sick mind at a sold-out stall rests instead (`finds the stall out

  seller's pouch grows, and the news reads `X bought medicine for
  Y — a family cares.`
- **Locked by test.** CoSaveTest carries a sold-down shelf
  (market 000250FE, 4 doses) through Encode → Decode and checks it

## 0.8.4 — Random Interactions (the trial)

**Goal:** settlers interact with each other unprompted — not only
when hunger drives them to a bench. The trial first: watch what the
existing sim already produces (trades at person-markets, rows at the
bench, the Socialize intent, proximity), run test trials, and
implement only what succeeds. The engine's patterns (Social/Trade
channels, `Say`, proximity reads) are the raw material; if the trial
shows the sim can't carry unprompted interaction cleanly, the stone
degrades to a documented finding and the run moves on.

**Status: VERIFIED 2026-08-14** — the trial passed, the stone ships.

**In-game:** a loaded settlement genuinely talks. 186 genuinely-near
speakers chattered at the sparse cadence (each mind ~once a minute,
jittered; the one-time wake greet wave reads as a settlement saying
good morning); the pools followed the bond (bonded caravan guards
got family lines, strangers got greet/gossip, no rows yet — no enemy
pair was near); walking minds never talked; and the speech stayed
local — log + subtitle-when-close, the feed untouched. The author
judged it "added life". The companions' own ambient lines observed
alongside were the vanilla game's dialogue coinciding — a bonus, not
ours. Two field fixes landed during the trial: the loaded gate
(`Get3D() != nullptr` — `GetFormByID` resolves streamed-out actors
whose positions read as garbage, which made the whole world look
"in range") and the cooldown-before-the-roll (a failed chance roll
retried next frame, making the gate meaningless). Tuning knobs
(`sim.interact.cadence/radius/chance`) are ready for the 0.9.1b
timings pass.

**What's built.** A proximity pass at the end of the per-second tick
(`Adapter::InteractPass`): loaded human minds gather their positions
once, and each cooldown-expired, non-walking mind finds its nearest
non-walking neighbour inside `sim.interact.radius` (default 400
units — a body's throw), rolls `sim.interact.chance` (0.4), and
speaks. The pool follows the bond via `Bonds::CurrentKind`: family
for spouse/sweetheart/friend, a quiet row for enemy/rival,
greet/gossip (30/70) for strangers. A walking mind never talks — the
interaction never interrupts a walk; a jittered cadence (0.5–1.5×
`sim.interact.cadence`, default 30 s) keeps a pair from lockstep
chatter. The speech is **local, not broadcast** (0.8.4 field truth):
it logs, and it subtitles only when the player is within
`sim.subtitle.radius` of the speaker — the settlement radio never
carries small talk, so the player hears what is said nearby, not the
whole Commonwealth's conversation.

**Files:** `src/Adapter.cpp` (`InteractPass` + the tick hook),
`src/Adapter.h` (the cooldown map), `src/Tuning.h` + the INI
(`sim.interact.cadence/radius/chance`), `Dialogue.h` (the pools it
speaks). Harness 26/26 green.

**Verification:** two settlers who cross paths sometimes greet, talk,
or trade without a hunger drive; the interaction is rate-limited,
never spammy, and doesn't interrupt a walk. If the trial fails, the
finding is written and the stone is closed as a cut. Its outcome
feeds the MCM page (0.8.5) and the audit (0.8.6a).

## 0.8.5 — MCM + Settings Manager

**Goal:** the player tunes in-game (Life.md's deferred player-window
half). A Mod Configuration Menu page exposing the tuning keys —
hunger rhythm, market hours, bond thresholds, births gate, illness
curve, and the interaction knobs the 0.8.4 trial proved. The INI
stays the source of truth; MCM writes an override file the adapter
overlays, with per-second hot-apply. The MCM mod is a **soft**
dependency.

**Status: VERIFIED 2026-08-14** — in-game test passed. The page
renders with real values (no NaN), a slider change hot-applies
within a second (`MCM override changed — hot-applied`), the
override survives a reload, and the stat-poll is quiet between
writes — 3 fires across a session (change + MCM's close-write +
a re-open), 0 errors. The Gestation label was renamed to
"Pregnancy length" so players don't need the medical term.

**What's built.** The MCM page (`MCM/Config/TheLivingCommonwealth/
config.json`, 6 pages / 41 controls: Life, Interactions, Relation-
ships, Illness, Birth & Fights, About) binds every player-facing
knob to MCM's ModSettings; the shipped defaults ride
`MCM/Settings/TheLivingCommonwealth.ini`. MCM stores the player's
changes in `Data\MCM\Settings\TheLivingCommonwealth.ini`, and the
adapter overlays that file on top of its own INI at load (the MCM
layer wins) **and** polls its last-write stamp once per second
(`CheckMcmOverride`), so a slider change lands in the sim within a
second — hot-applied, no rebuild.

**The first field fix (NaN reads).** The first in-game pass showed
the page but every control read NaN — MCM's settings store is
**typed**, so a bare `sim.hunger.decay` key parses as nothing.
Fixed: the settings file now uses MCM's typed keys (`f`/`i`/`b`
prefixes), the page's controls bind by `id` (`"fHungerDecay:Main"`
+ `sourceType ModSettingFloat/Int/Bool`), the adapter strips the
type prefix when overlaying, and an **About page** was added. The
JSON and settings file are re-read by MCM on menu open, so this
half of the fix landed live; the DLL half (prefix-strip) deploys
with the next restart.

**The honest design note:** no native Papyrus bridge. This pin's
`IVirtualMachine` exposes no `RegisterFunction` surface (62 virtuals,
stripped header), and hand-declaring the vtable entry blind is a
hard-crash risk at load. The override-file + stat-poll achieves the
same player-facing result (in-game editing, persists, applies
fast) with zero crash risk.

**Files:** `MCM/Config/TheLivingCommonwealth/config.json`,
`MCM/Settings/TheLivingCommonwealth.ini` (author-side assets),
`src/Adapter.h/.cpp` (`m_Config` + the overlay + `CheckMcmOverride`
in the per-second tick), `src/Tuning.h` (unchanged — the INI stays
the schema). Harness 26/26 green.

**Verification:** the page shows in the MCM under The Living
Commonwealth; a slider change shows up in the sim within a second
(the `tuning: MCM override changed — hot-applied` log line) and
survives a reload; with no MCM installed, the INI alone still
works.
